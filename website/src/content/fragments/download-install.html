After you download

Three steps, then it's in your menu bar.

1. Unzip. Double-click Pace-{{version}}.zip. You'll get Pace.app.

2. Open it. Double-click Pace.app. If macOS blocks it, follow the Privacy & Security approval above. Once Pace opens, review its Microphone, Screen Recording, and Accessibility requests for voice, screen context, and approved actions.

3. Hold ctrl+option and talk. That's push-to-talk. Pace measures time to first spoken response on every turn and lives in your menu bar from now on — no dock icon.

This preview is not Developer ID signed or notarized. See Apple's current opening instructions for the per-app approval in Privacy & Security. The Open Anyway option is available for about an hour after an opening attempt.
