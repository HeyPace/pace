Choose the conversation model you want

Cursor Voice documents OpenAI Realtime voice sessions, interruption by speaking, screen tools and an optional on-device wake word. Its privacy section says active-session audio goes to OpenAI while dismissed-orb audio does not. A local wake word and Keychain-stored API key do not turn that conversation into on-device inference.

Investigate Cursor Voice when conversational voice and interruption matter and a remote audio path fits your requirements. Review its current permissions and installation caveats before setup.

HeyPace's 0.3.20 release acceptance is text-first, with voice and hotkey acceptance unverified. Its local-model and explicitly selected external-planner options serve a different evaluation. Do not choose this public preview on an assumed proven voice advantage or a blanket zero-cost claim.

Compare input and output separately

Use the same non-sensitive question with a known answer. In Cursor Voice, check the transcript or visible reply, spoken reply and whether interruption stops the response. In HeyPace, establish a typed-answer control first; mark any untested voice stage unknown. Record provider, network state and failures. An answer displayed on screen is not a successful spoken conversation.
