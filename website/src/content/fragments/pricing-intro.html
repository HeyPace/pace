Pricing

Try it free. Buy it once. Studio comes later.

Pace is fully on-device — the inference costs nothing to run, so there's no per-turn bill to pass on. Pace is a $29 one-time purchase, currently handled through a manual email path when hosted checkout is not configured. The downloadable preview remains free.

Try the whole app free first. If your Mac has Apple Intelligence, Pace runs against Apple's Foundation Models at no cost. Buy the $29 purchase only after you've decided the bundled stack is worth it.
