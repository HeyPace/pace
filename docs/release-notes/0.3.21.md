## Chat and powers

- Voice and typed messages share one main chat. Reply to a message to start a thread. Pace keeps its draft, queued requests, and replies together when you switch conversations.
- Open **Powers** in the chat header to search built-in actions, saved powers, installed workflows, and configured connections. Search for **Chrome** to find a configured Browse connection. Test the connection to inspect the tools it advertises, or choose **Use in chat** to prepare an editable request.
- Open **Usage** from the chat options menu to see failures and latency, including P99. You can inspect individual powers and workflows.

## Desktop controls

- The chat composer brings typing, microphone controls, queueing, and stopping a request into one place.
- Pace asks for confirmation when a spoken power name is ambiguous. Catalog selections are checked against the current inventory before execution.
- Settings shows the same managed power inventory as chat. Deleting a saved power asks for confirmation and reports errors.
